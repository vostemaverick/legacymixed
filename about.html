About Us

Legacy Mixed Secondary School is located in taasia, Nairobi. Founded on the belief that every student has potential, our motto "I Know I Can" inspires learners to believe in themselves.

Our Mission

To provide quality education that builds character, academic excellence and self-belief.

Our Vision
